Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's most famous paintings. It shows a beautiful night sky with swirling stars above a quiet village. The painting is known for its expressive brush strokes and vibrant colors.
